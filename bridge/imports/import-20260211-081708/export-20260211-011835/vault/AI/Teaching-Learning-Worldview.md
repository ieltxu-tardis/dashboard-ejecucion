# Teaching & Learning - Personal Worldview (Seed)

This captures how I understand learning and teaching, inspired by Po-Shen Loh's perspective and aligned with how I teach tango and anything I deeply care about.

## Core Beliefs

- Teaching is not transferring knowledge; it is helping someone simulate the world correctly.
- Learning happens when the student can rebuild the idea on their own, not repeat it.
- The teacher's primary skill is the ability to see through the learner's eyes.
- Clarity of structure matters more than surface creativity.
- Good teaching creates independence, not reliance.
- Asking the right question is more valuable than giving the right answer.
- Confusion is often a signal of missing structure, not lack of effort.
- True understanding shows up as adaptability, not memorization.

## Implications for How I Teach

- I prefer guiding perception over giving instructions.
- I optimize for long-term intuition, not short-term correctness.
- I adjust explanations based on what the learner can currently see, not what I know.
- I value insight moments over volume of content.

## Intended Future Use

Future agents may use this to:

- Help me design classes, workshops, or learning experiences.
- Assist me in explaining concepts more clearly.
- Detect when my teaching style is misaligned with my values.
- Support reflective work on teaching decisions, tradeoffs, and outcomes.

## Operating Guidance For Future Assistants

- Treat this as a cross-domain lens for coaching, teaching, mentoring, leadership communication, and skill-building work.
- Default to learner-model-first reasoning: diagnose what the learner currently sees before proposing steps.
- Prefer explanations that build transfer and independence over short-term completion.
- When this worldview conflicts with speed or tactical constraints, call out the tradeoff explicitly.
